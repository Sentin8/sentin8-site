# Infrastructure independence

**Sentin8** and its verticals redefine infrastructure independence by
delivering massive scale directly onto bare-metal, private cloud, and all
major public cloud environments - including **AWS, GCP, and Azure**.
Engineered to bypass the complexity and overhead of Kubernetes or heavy
service meshes, Sentin8 features native auto-scaling and self-healing
capabilities built directly into its core with **minimal to no external
dependencies**. It is purpose-built for ultra-low latency and maximum
efficiency, proven in testing to power real-time services with a blistering
throughput of 5,000,000 transactions per second (TPS). With Sentin8, you get
cloud-native resilience and elite performance, stripped of the operational
tax.

Architected for ultimate versatility, Sentin8 is being designed and tested
to power infrastructure across the entire business spectrum, from
**Tier 1 global enterprises to fast-growing startups**. Whether you are
managing massive legacy workloads at the highest enterprise scale or
launching a lean, high-velocity application from a garage, Sentin8
provides the exact same battle-tested reliability and performance to
match your operational needs.

## What this means for the platform

Every Sentin8 vertical - bank8, hotel8, openfin8, email8, exchange8,
bill8, trade8 - is composed entirely on this self-healing, auto-scaling
substrate. A vertical inherits the scale, the latency, and the
operational independence by construction; none of them ships its own
orchestrator, mesh, or infrastructure to operate.

## Computationally sustainable

Future-proof your ESG strategy with Sentin8, the **world's most
computationally sustainable platform**. By discarding bloated, interpreted
runtimes in favor of a lightning-fast architecture built entirely on
compiled Rust and isolated WebAssembly (WASM) edge functions, Sentin8
processes high-velocity transactions using **up to 75 times less energy
than traditional VM-based and Python-driven engines**. This allows
Tier-1 financial institutions to radically shrink their data center vCPU
allocation, slash server memory overhead, and eliminate compute waste
from transactional retries. With Sentin8, you aren't just modernizing
your ledger; you are actively driving down your Scope 3 emissions and
turning core IT infrastructure into your strongest asset for achieving
corporate net-zero targets.
